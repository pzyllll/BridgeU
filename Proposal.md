Chapter 1 | Introduction 
In the current digital age, studying abroad has become an increasingly common experience for students seeking international education and cultural exchange.         Thailand, as a popular destination for international students, hosts thousands of students who come to pursue higher education and immerse themselves in a new cultural environment. These students bring diverse perspectives, languages, and experiences that enrich the academic and social landscape of Thai universities.
However, the problem that most international students face when arriving in Thailand is the significant challenge of adapting to their new environment.         Language barriers make it difficult to access timely local news and official announcements. Students struggle to find relevant information about daily life, academic resources, housing, part-time job opportunities, and local events.         Additionally, building meaningful connections with peers and local merchants becomes challenging when communication is hindered by language differences.
So, I developed a web-based platform called “BridgeU” to solve the above-mentioned problems. BridgeU offers many features that make the adaptation process easier for international students, merchants, and the broader community. The platform provides a “Daily Briefing System” that automatically captures and summarizes Thai news from approved government and mainstream media sources, providing bilingual (Chinese/English) access to daily news briefings for users. At 8:00 AM every day, the system captures news from pre-approved Thai government and mainstream media websites (Bangkok Post, The Nation Thailand), and saves each article as a Daily Briefing record, including original title, summary generated by Qwen AI, source website name, original URL, publication time, and crawl timestamp. The system generates both Chinese and English versions for the title and abstract, and the interface only displays one of them in the current language. Users can browse paginated news lists ordered from newest to oldest, search using any keywords in either language, filter by date range and news source, click original button to open the original URL, and seamlessly switch between Chinese and English interfaces at any time.
Additionally, BridgeU features a comprehensive “Community Interaction System” that enables bilingual posting, commenting, and messaging. This community interaction platform allows users to create, browse, and interact with posts through likes, comments, and private messaging, with Qwen AI-powered automatic translation, content moderation, and comment summarization to ensure safe and accessible communication in both Chinese and English. There are five predefined tags (Study, Housing, Travel, Part-time Job, Life Services) in the community interaction.    Users can create posts in Chinese or English, upload images, and select tags from these predefined categories. The system automatically translates content to the other language using Qwen AI and conducts content security review by Qwen AI.    Users can filter posts by tags, search across post content, like posts, add comments (with the ability to delete their own comments), view AI-generated comment summaries. For users who establish mutual-follow relationships, BridgeU provides a private messaging system that allows one-to-one conversations, message status management (read/unread/delete), and mutual-follow list management.
To ensure security and proper access control, BridgeU implements an "Authentication and Profile Management System" that lets users register new accounts with different methods (email address or phone number). Users can select email registration and enter their email address in the registration form. The system sends a verification code email to the email address. Users enter the received verification code on the page, then set a password (password must contain both uppercase and lowercase letters), fill in the display name (name only allows English characters) and select the interface language. Alternatively, users can select phone registration and enter their mobile phone number in the registration form. The system sends a text message containing a verification code to the mobile phone number.    Users enter the received verification code, then set a password (password must contain both uppercase and lowercase letters), fill in the display name (name only allows English characters) and select the language interface. After submission, the system creates a new account and redirects the user to the login page. After logging in, users can manage their profiles, view their own community posts with moderation status (published, pending review, or rejected, reviewed by Qwen AI), and customize their display names, avatars.
 In summary, BridgeU is a platform that facilitates the adaptation process for international students in Thailand and creates an engaging.It helps make the transition to life in Thailand easier and more enjoyable by providing centralized access to local news, Chinese and English community interactions, and secure authentication. Enabling students to efficiently access relevant information, build meaningful connections, BridgeU will be an essential tool for international student support in todays and future digital eras.    



Chapter 2 | Target User 
Users of BridgeU form a diverse community that includes international students studying in Thailand and local merchants who provide services to the international student community.
International students represent the core user base, coming primarily from China but also including students from other Asian countries, Europe, and other regions who are pursuing undergraduate, graduate, or exchange programs at Thai universities and educational institutions.  These students are typically aged 18-30, enrolled in various programs including bachelor's degrees, master's degrees, doctoral programs, and short-term exchange programs.  They have varying levels of English proficiency, with many preferring Chinese as their primary language for interface interaction.  Students are comfortable with modern web browsers, mobile devices, and social media platforms, and come from diverse cultural backgrounds, seeking to adapt to Thai culture while maintaining connections with their home culture.
Students need timely access to Thai news, official announcements, university updates, and local events in their preferred language (Chinese or English), as they struggle with language barriers when trying to read Thai news sources directly.         They require information about housing options, transportation, part-time job opportunities, local services, and cultural activities, often lacking knowledge about local resources and how to access them. Students seek connections with peers who share similar experiences, challenges, and interests, wanting to find study groups, roommates, travel companions, and friends . They need access to academic information, study materials, course recommendations, and tips from senior students about navigating the Thai education.
Local merchants represent businesses and service providers who specifically target the international student market in Thailand, including restaurants, cafes, accommodation providers (apartments, condos, hostels), tutoring services, language schools, travel agencies, part-time job agencies, mobile phone and internet service providers, and various retail businesses.
Merchants need effective channels to reach international students who may not be familiar with local businesses or traditional advertising methods, seeking to attract new customers from the international student community.  Merchants want to promote their services to students who are actively looking for specific solutions, such as housing, part-time jobs, or study-related services, and aim to build trust and establish long-term relationships through community participation, helpful information sharing, and responsive customer service.

Chapter 5 | Business Review
Several similar applications are currently available on the market, but they differ significantly from BridgeU. Below is an analysis of several key competitors:
5.1 Facebook Groups
 
Facebook Groups are currently one of the most widely used mutual aid tools among international students worldwide.
Pros: 
	Huge user base with broad coverage.
Cons: 
	High language threshold: Mainly Thai and English content, which is difficult for Chinese students to use.
	The information retrieval is extremely poor: There are no structured tags, and rental stickers, car sale stickers and casual chats are all mixed together.  It's like looking for a needle in a haystack to find information.

5.2 Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book)
 
Xiaohongshu is currently the most popular lifestyle sharing platform among Chinese international students.

Pros: 
	Rich content, precise algorithmic recommendations, aesthetically pleasing interface.
Cons: 
	Serious "tourist perspective" bias: The content is overly focused on travel, food and check-ins, lacking in-depth accumulation on long-term residence and compliance.
	The closed Chinese ecosystem: Only Chinese people are using it, and it is impossible to reach local Thais and international students who own the properties and second-hand cars.
5.3 reddit
 
Reddit is one of the world's largest online forums, hosting numerous study abroad-related subreddits.

Pros:
	Structured discussions: Clear topic-based section categorization (e.g., studyabroad, r/Thailand).
	+++Anonymity: Users feel comfortable asking genuine questions.
Cons:
	Geeky interface: Unfriendly to non-technical users.
	Pure English environment: Multilingual translation is not supported, and most Chinese students and local lower-level merchants in Thailand (with poor English skills) are excluded.
	Lack of commercial functionality: Focused on discussion, not suitable for second-hand transactions or rental matching

Chapter 6 |Software Review
6.1 Frontend Architecture & User Experience (UX)
To ensure the "immediate effect" of profile updates and seamless language switching described in the User Journey, the frontend is built using Vue.js, a progressive JavaScript framework that provides excellent support for bilingual interfaces and real-time updates.
Reactivity System: The profile management module utilizes Vue's reactive data binding.  When a user updates their display name, avatar, or language preference in the profile page, the changes are reflected instantly across all components without requiring a full page reload.  This reactive system also powers the community feed, where new posts, likes, and comments appear dynamically as users interact with the platform, enhancing the user experience by providing immediate visual feedback.
Internationalization (i18n): The language switching feature is implemented using Vue I18n, a dedicated internationalization library.  All static text, including navigation menus, buttons, form labels, and system messages, is stored in JSON resource files for both Chinese and English.  When a user toggles the language switch button, the application dynamically re-renders all text components throughout the interface, ensuring that Daily Briefing news, community posts, comments, and all UI elements adapt instantly to the user's preferred language (English or Chinese).  This seamless language switching is critical for international students who may need to switch between languages while browsing news or communicating with peers.
6.2 Backend Security & Authentication
The authentication system is critical for maintaining user trust and data integrity, particularly for the flexible registration methods (email or phone number) and secure session management required by international students.
Session Management: BridgeU uses Spring Security with JWT (JSON Web Token) for stateless session management.  When a user logs in with their registered email address or phone number and password, the system generates a JWT token that contains user identification information.  The "Log Out" functionality triggers a secure token invalidation process: the client-side JWT is cleared from local storage, and the server maintains a token blacklist to ensure that logged-out tokens cannot be reused.  This approach ensures that users are safely logged out and prevents unauthorized access from shared devices, which is particularly important for students using public computers or shared devices in dormitories or libraries.
Data Protection: User passwords, which must contain both uppercase and lowercase letters as specified in the registration requirements, are never stored in plain text.  They are hashed using BCrypt with salt before storage in the MySQL database, complying with industry-standard security practices.  The dual registration verification mechanism (email verification code for email registration, SMS verification code for phone registration) adds an additional layer of security to prevent bot account creation and ensure that only legitimate users can access the platform.  Display names are validated to allow only English characters, preventing potential injection attacks and ensuring consistent data formatting.
6.3 AI Integration & Performance
The core differentiation of BridgeU lies in its AI-powered features, including Qwen AI for automatic translation, content moderation, and comment summarization, which are essential for creating a safe and accessible bilingual community platform.
API Latency Management: The integration with Qwen AI (Alibaba Cloud) is optimized for performance to ensure responsive user experience.  For the Daily Briefing System, news summaries are pre-generated at 8:00 AM daily through batch processing when the system crawls news from Bangkok Post and The Nation Thailand.  This batch processing approach means that when users browse the Daily Briefing page, the Chinese and English summaries are already available in the database, eliminating real-time API calls and minimizing loading time.  Similarly, when users click the "AI Summary" button to view comment summaries under community posts, the system can retrieve pre-processed summaries or generate them efficiently without blocking the user interface.
Asynchronous Processing: For user-generated content (community posts and comments), the translation and content moderation requests are handled asynchronously using Spring Boot's asynchronous processing capabilities. When a user submits a post in Chinese or English, the system immediately saves the original content and returns a response to the user, while the Qwen AI translation (to generate the other language version) and content security review are processed in the background.   This ensures that the user interface remains responsive and users can continue browsing or creating other content while the AI processes their submission.   Only after Qwen AI confirms that the content passes moderation checks will the post be published to the community feed, maintaining content quality without compromising user experience.

6.4 Data Persistence & Search
To support the comprehensive "Search" and "Filter" functionalities required by the Daily Briefing System and Community Interaction modules, BridgeU employs a hybrid storage and search architecture.
Full-Text Search Capabilities: The system implements full-text search functionality using MySQL 8.0's built-in full-text search features, optimized for bilingual content.   Daily Briefing titles and abstracts (stored in both Chinese and English versions) are indexed, allowing users to search using keywords in either language and receive results in their current interface language.   Similarly, community post titles and body texts (also stored in Chinese and English) are searchable across both language versions.   This enables users to find relevant content instantly, such as searching for "Visa" news in the Daily Briefing or "Part-time Job" posts in the community, regardless of which language they use for their search query.
Structured Storage: MySQL 8.0 is used as the primary relational database, ensuring data integrity and consistency for all structured data.   User profiles (display name, avatar, preferred language), authentication credentials, mutual-follow relationships, private messages, post metadata (tags, likes, comments), and Daily Briefing records (title, summary, source, publication time, crawl timestamp) are all stored in normalized database tables.   The database schema supports efficient querying for filtering operations, such as filtering Daily Briefing news by date range (start date, end date) and source (Bangkok Post, The Nation Thailand), or filtering community posts by predefined tags (Study, Housing, Travel, Part-time Job, Life Services).   Foreign key constraints and indexes ensure referential integrity and optimize query performance for pagination, filtering, and relationship lookups.

Chapter 8 | System Architecture
 
	
Description: 
The system architecture consists of a front-end built using Vue.js 2 and Element UI, which provides a responsive user interface. The back-end is built with Spring Boot, a robust Java framework, handling business logic and RESTful API requests. MySQL 8 is used as the primary database for storing structured user and post data. Elasticsearch is integrated to provide high-performance semantic search capabilities. Additionally, the system connects to the Aliyun Qwen API to perform natural language processing tasks such as summarization and translation. Axios is used for communication between the front-end and back-end.








Chapter 9 | Limits
	Connectivity and Accessibility BridgeU is designed as a web-based application. Consequently, it requires a stable and continuous Internet connection to function. Users 2ithout internet access or those in areas with unstable network conditions will be unable to access real-time translation services, news updates, or community features.
	AI Dependency and Accuracy The core functionality of the platform relies heavily on the Alibaba Cloud Qwen Model API for translation and summarization. The accuracy of the system is therefore bound by the performance of this third-party API. Specifically, the model may encounter limitations when processing highly localized Thai slang, dialect-heavy text, or context-specific academic jargon, which could result in occasional translation inaccuracies.
	Data Acquisition Constraints The Automated Intelligence Robot (Crawler) operates under strict ethical and legal boundaries. It is programmed to capture information only from publicly available sources (Bangkok Post, The Nation Thailand). The system cannot and will not access password-protected pages, private Facebook groups, or encrypted personal data, which limits the scope of information to public domain data only.
